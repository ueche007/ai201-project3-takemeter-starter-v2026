# Copied from the README so the baseline is judged on exactly the
# definitions in the taxonomy. baseline.py reads this file.

question: The writer asks or requests something from readers (advice, information, a recommendation or an opinion), and getting answers is the reason for the post.
firsthand: The writer reports something that happened in their own running (a race, a run, a training block, an injury, or what happened when they tried something) with at least one specific detail tying it to a particular occasion.
claim: The writer tells readers what to do, or states what is true about running, races, gear or runners, without a firsthand account of their own behind it.
chatter: Social talk that says nothing about running: agreement, thanks, congratulations, jokes, or talk about Reddit and the subreddit.
